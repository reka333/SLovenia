Our story

Three years, one long weekend, and the most beautiful lake in the Alps.

Our anniversary is on Thursday, 22 October. The next morning at six we load Odi in Dunakeszi and head for the Julian Alps, with lunch in Maribor on the way. In Breg, right next to Bled, a little apartment is waiting for us: baguettes for breakfast, pesto pasta for dinner, pancakes one evening, and nobody rushing us anywhere.

This weekend is about slowing down. A gorge, two lakes, a stroll through Ljubljana, F1 on the sofa and a lot of time together. If it rains, we have a cosy plan B for every day.

Happy third anniversary.

    On our route

    Places worth seeing

    The highlights of the plan, plus a couple of extras in case the weather is kinder than expected.

    Friday · ~15 min from Breg

    Lake Bled

    An afternoon walk along the shore with the island church in view.

    Saturday · seasonal

    Vintgar Gorge

    A 1.6 km boardwalk above the Radovna river, ending at the Šum waterfall. Tickets online only.

    Sunday · ~40 min

    Lake Bohinj

    Slovenia's largest permanent natural lake, with the stone bridge and St John's church at Ribčev Laz.

    Rainy-day option

    Bled Castle

    First mentioned in 1011. Its indoor exhibitions are a good plan B on a wet Saturday.

    Extra · ~30 min

    Lake Jasna

    An alpine lake near Kranjska Gora, only if there's a spare sunny hour.

    Maribor, Lent

    The old riverside quarter on the Drava, our Friday lunch stop.

    Mostnica Gorge

    A narrow gorge above Stara Fužina, a few minutes from Lake Bohinj. Only on a safe, dry-ish path.

    Radovljica

    A small old town with painted houses and the Beekeeping Museum, next to Lesce.

    Ljubljana old town

    Prešeren Square, the Triple Bridge and the Ljubljanica embankments on Monday.

    Practical

    Good to know

    Slovenian motorway vignette

    Only an e-vignette exists, linked to your number plate. For a car (class 2A) the 7-day one is enough and costs €16. Buy it on the official evinjeta.dars.si, since resellers add a fee. Type the plate exactly, or it is invalid.

    Hungarian section

    The M7 needs a vignette too: a national one, or county ones for Pest, Fejér, Somogy and Zala on the way from Dunakeszi.

    Clocks go back

    On Sunday 25 October at 3:00 clocks go back to 2:00. From Sunday it gets dark around 17:00.

    Shops shut on Sunday

    Most shops close on Sundays and holidays. Small shops at petrol stations are an exception.

    Driving in Slovenia

    Dipped headlights are required during the day too. Winter equipment is mandatory from 15 November, but may be needed earlier in snow.

    Parking at Bled

    Lakeside car parks are paid and fill up quickly. Arrive early, or park further out and walk.

    Weather

    About 10–15 °C by day, 0–5 °C at night. Foggy mornings, rain possible, snow up high.

    Little things

    Euro, cards accepted almost everywhere. Tap water is drinkable. Same plugs and time zone as Hungary. Emergency number: 112.
