# Szlovénia Holiday 2026

Our 3rd-anniversary road trip with Odi to Breg, next to Lake Bled, 23–26 October 2026.

Open `index.html` in a browser. The page has the day-by-day route map, the itinerary with rainy-day plans, places, cosy evening ideas, and packing and shopping lists.

- `index.html` – page content
- `style.css` – design, light and dark theme
- `script.js` – route map, swipe rails, checklists, animations
- `img/` – photos from Unsplash (Unsplash License)

Route lines and drive times: OpenStreetMap (OSRM), country and lake outlines: OpenStreetMap (Nominatim), © OpenStreetMap contributors.
